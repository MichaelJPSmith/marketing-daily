ESA Kicks Off IRIS² Next Phase
Europe  |  Government / Space Agency
Industry News
ESA has signed 18 contracts worth roughly €20m with an industrial consortium of 80 companies, research institutes and SMEs across Europe to advance the next phase of IRIS², the EU's secure multi-orbit satellite constellation. The work covers Low-LEO architecture, resilient comms links and quantum-resistant cryptographic payloads - building on the €22.1bn Member States committed at CM25.
🔗 Source: ESA

Europe's sovereign satellite network just got 80 companies deep. ESA has kicked off the next phase of IRIS², its multi-orbit secure connectivity constellation - signing 18 contracts worth roughly €20m with an industrial consortium spanning 80 companies, research institutes and SMEs across Europe. The work covers Low-LEO architecture, resilient communications links and quantum-resistant cryptographic payloads, building directly on the €22.1bn Member States committed at CM25. This is exactly the kind of procurement moment commercial space companies need to be in the room for - a government-backed programme opening its supply chain to dozens of new industrial partners.
