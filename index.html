GomSpace
Denmark / Sweden  |  Small satellites
Mission
GomSpace's GOMX-5, an ESA-supported 8U CubeSat, is set to fly on SpaceX's Transporter-18 rideshare to demonstrate next-generation satellite communications and sensing technologies in low Earth orbit. It will also show collision avoidance and end-of-life disposal in line with ESA's Zero Debris initiative.

GomX-5 is on its way to orbit. GomSpace has announced its ESA-supported 8U CubeSat, which will fly on SpaceX's Transporter-18 rideshare mission. It will demonstrate next-generation satellite communications and sensing technologies in low Earth orbit, and prove out collision avoidance and end-of-life disposal in line with ESA's Zero Debris initiative. It follows the GOMX-4 pair, which monitored maritime and air traffic over the Arctic. Small, capable and responsible - exactly where the industry is heading. BAE Systems
UK  |  Space systems
Programme Milestone
BAE Systems has completed the Critical Design Review for MEO Epoch 2, a $1.2 billion US Space Force Space Systems Command programme. BAE is prime contractor, building 10 medium Earth orbit satellites plus the ground system to run the constellation. The review landed on schedule, 14 months after award.
🔗 Source: Option Finance
👤 LinkedIn Post
A big milestone for a big space programme. BAE Systems has completed the Critical Design Review for MEO Epoch 2, a $1.2 billion US Space Force programme. As prime contractor, BAE is building 10 medium Earth orbit satellites and the ground system to manage the constellation. The review finished on schedule, 14 months after award, helped by buying long-lead components early. Delivery discipline like that is what makes space programmes work.
