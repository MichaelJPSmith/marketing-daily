Teledyne Space Imaging has been showcasing its sensor and camera portfolio this month, including the new space-ready camera built around its Emerald 67M CMOS sensor - high resolution, high dynamic range, low noise, global shutter. The company also supplied the two CCD370 imaging sensors for the Soft X-ray Imager aboard ESA's SMILE mission, which launched from French Guiana earlier this year. Detectors are where the science actually happens. Every image that comes back from orbit is only as good as the silicon that caught the photons.
