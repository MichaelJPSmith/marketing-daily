South Africa  |  Earth Observation Payloads
Contract Win
Canadian Earth observation company Galaxia has acquired one of Simera Sense's HyperScape100 hyperspectral imagers - a compact payload built for small satellites that pulls 32 user-selectable spectral bands from a library of 400. A neat example of how specialist payload makers are quietly becoming the backbone of the next generation of EO constellations.

Another win for South African space imaging. Simera Sense has confirmed that Canadian Earth observation company Galaxia has acquired one of its HyperScape100 hyperspectral imagers - a compact payload that can pull 32 user-selectable spectral bands from a library of 400, built specifically for small satellites. It's a neat example of how specialist payload makers are quietly becoming the backbone of the next generation of EO constellations. Space Park Leicester
UK  |  Space Research Campus
Policy Reaction
Space Park Leicester has welcomed the UK's new National Space Strategy, published 8 September with £7.8bn committed through to 2030. The strategy's priority areas - Earth Observation, Positioning Navigation and Timing, and In-Orbit Servicing, Assembly and Manufacturing - map directly onto research built up over six decades at the University of Leicester.
