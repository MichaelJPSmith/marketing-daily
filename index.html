Europe is taking a serious look at its own space station. ESA has selected OHB to lead one of two parallel consortia studying a European-led low Earth orbit outpost, alongside a competing team led by Airbus. The studies will inform proposals ESA takes to member states at the December ministerial meeting, and build on European heritage from the Columbus module and the Orion European Service Module.
