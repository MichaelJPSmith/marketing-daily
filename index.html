Europe just got a new space unicorn. Open Cosmos, the Oxfordshire-based satellite manufacturer and operator, has closed a EUR300m ($348m) Series C round - co-led by Lightrock and ETF Partners, with Claret Capital Partners providing a venture debt tranche. The raise pushes Open Cosmos past a $1 billion valuation, making it one of Europe's fastest-growing satellite businesses and a strong signal of investor confidence in the continent's space sector. Skyrora
UK  |  Launch / Propulsion
Milestone Test
Skyrora has completed a hot-fire test campaign of its 3.5kN LEO engine, featuring a titanium-coated component developed through the EU-funded MADE-3D Horizon Europe programme, at its Midlothian testing facility.
🔗 Source: TCT Magazine
👤 LinkedIn Post
3D-printed rocket engines just passed a major test. Skyrora has completed a hot-fire test campaign of its 3.5kN LEO engine, featuring a titanium-coated component developed through the EU-funded MADE-3D programme, a Horizon Europe additive manufacturing initiative. Carried out at Skyrora's Midlothian facility, the test marks a significant milestone in bringing next-generation manufacturing techniques into the space sector - and wraps up the company's work within the international MADE-3D consortium.
