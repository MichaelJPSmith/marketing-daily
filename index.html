UK Military Satcom & Unified Procurement
UK  |  Government / Defence
Industry News
The UK Space Strategy commits £2.3bn to military satellite communications to 2029/30 and moves government to a single approach to buying space technology, starting with satcom (SKYNET and Connectivity in Low Earth Orbit). The stated aim is to help British firms win contracts and strengthen UK supply chains.
🔗 Source: Defence Industry Europe (citing the UK Space Strategy)
👤 LinkedIn Post
The UK is putting £2.3bn behind military satellite communications to 2030 - and changing how government buys space technology. Under the new UK Space Strategy, government will take a single approach to buying and developing space tech, starting with satellite communications. That covers SKYNET and Connectivity in Low Earth Orbit, with a stated aim of helping British firms win contracts and strengthening UK supply chains. For commercial space companies chasing their first or next government contract, one joined-up buyer is a big deal. This is exactly the kind of procurement shift they need to be in the room for - which is exactly what Space-Comm Expo Europe exists to deliver. EDA Space Data Mechanism
Europe  |  Defence / Space Domain Awareness
Industry News
Fifteen EU countries have agreed a European Defence Agency mechanism to share information on objects and activity in orbit, protecting satellites that deliver imagery, navigation and communications. Initial funding is ~€50m, pay-as-you-go, with capability targeted for 2029 and a supplier assessment survey running to December.
🔗 Source: Defence Industry Europe (citing the European Defence Agency)
👤 LinkedIn Post
Fifteen European nations have agreed to share space data to protect their satellites. Through the European Defence Agency, a new mechanism will give participating countries shared information on objects and activity in orbit, supporting satellites that deliver imagery, navigation and communications. Initial funding is ~€50m, on a pay-as-you-go model with no long-term commitments, and a supplier assessment survey runs to December. A European buyer asking suppliers what they can offer is a real door for commercial space companies. This is exactly the kind of procurement moment they need to be in the room for - which is exactly what Space-Comm Expo Europe exists to deliver.
