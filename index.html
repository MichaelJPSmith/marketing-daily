MDA Space has just laid out the full MDA CHORUS portfolio, days before its SAR constellation heads to orbit. Three offerings - COMMAND, COMMAND PRO and INTELLIGENCE - give customers control over their geointelligence workflow, from tasking the satellites to analysing the data. The constellation pairs two new spacecraft with the operational RADARSAT-2, and the new CHORUS Control Centre in Quebec is already open. Maritime awareness, vessel detection and infrastructure monitoring are all in scope, built on decades of SAR expertise. Proud to have MDA Space exhibiting at Space-Comm Expo Europe 2027.
