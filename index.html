The UK just stood up its first dedicated satellite protection unit. At this week's UK Space Power Conference, the Royal Air Force announced No. III Space Effects Squadron - the UK's first military unit focused specifically on protecting the satellites the country now depends on for connectivity, navigation and communications. It's a clear signal that space domain awareness and satellite resilience technology are becoming a standing government priority, not a one-off project. This is exactly the kind of procurement shift commercial space companies need to be in the room for - which is exactly what Space-Comm Expo Europe exists to deliver.
