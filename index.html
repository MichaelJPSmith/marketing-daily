Big funding news out of Bulgaria's space sector this week. EnduroSat has closed a $205 million round, co-led by Riot Ventures and Atreides Management, to scale satellite production across Europe and the US. The raise funds a new high-volume manufacturing facility in the US and the EU's largest space and defence hub, and backs EnduroSat's push to bring the cost of space data down to roughly $1 per gigabyte - a shift that could finally make satellite data affordable for SMEs, not just governments and primes. Viasat
USA  |  Satellite Communications
Partnership
Viasat and Space42 have signed a binding agreement to co-found Equatys, a shared space and ground infrastructure platform for direct-to-device connectivity, with the two companies committing up to $1B in initial equity. The platform will be supported by up to ~2,800 satellites.
🔗 Source: Payload Space
👤 LinkedIn Post
When two space companies commit up to $1 billion to build shared infrastructure together, it's worth paying attention. Viasat and Space42 have signed a binding agreement to co-found Equatys - a shared space and ground infrastructure platform designed to support up to ~2,800 satellites, connecting smartphones and IoT devices directly where terrestrial networks can't reach. Viasat is set to be appointed prime technology contractor as the venture moves toward launch. Proud to have Viasat exhibiting at Space-Comm Expo Europe 2027.
