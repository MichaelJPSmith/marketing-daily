$68.1 million. That's the size of Filtronic's latest order from SpaceX, the largest single order the company has ever received from them. The order is for Filtronic's Cerus E-band GaN solid-state power amplifiers, built for low Earth orbit satellite communications. GaN offers more output power, better efficiency and better thermal performance than older GaAs designs, and this UK-designed technology is now being scaled for delivery through FY2028. European Space Investment: ESA Earth Observation & Intelligence Constellation
Europe  |  Government / Defence
Industry News
ESA is seeking to lift funding for the European Earth observation and intelligence constellation from EUR167m to EUR350m. Governments have until 30 November to subscribe, in-orbit demonstration selection concludes in December, and Leonardo and an Iceye-led consortium hold parallel architecture design contracts (awarded 21 September).
🔗 Source: SatNews
👤 LinkedIn Post
Europe is lining up its next big space intelligence capability, and the numbers are moving fast. ESA has asked for its funding for the European Earth observation and intelligence constellation to rise from EUR167 million to EUR350 million, covering payload development, secure communications links and launch. Governments have until 30 November to subscribe, and the selection of in-orbit demonstration missions concludes in December. Leonardo and an Iceye-led consortium are already working on parallel architecture designs. For commercial space companies chasing their first or next government contract, this is exactly the kind of procurement moment you need to be in the room for, which is exactly what Space-Comm Expo Europe exists to deliver.
