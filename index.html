🚀 £12 million has just opened up for UK space companies looking to deepen their international partnerships. The UK Space Agency's International Bilateral Fund - Call 3 offers up to £1.5 million per project for ambitious space collaborations, with partnerships in Canada, France, Germany, India, Japan and the USA especially encouraged. Between 8 and 12 projects will be funded, each running up to 18 months from April 2027. It's a reminder that government backing for the space sector isn't just about the big headline strategy numbers - it's also about the practical funding routes that open doors to new international partners and markets.
