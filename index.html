45 seconds of fire on the Kintyre coast, and Britain's homegrown orbital rocket just took another step forward. Skyrora has completed a static hot-fire test of a scaled-down Skyrora XL at Machrihanish in Scotland. The test gathered data on pressurisation, propellant flow rate and chamber pressure, and all subsystems performed successfully. The XL is designed to carry small satellites into low Earth orbit, with additive manufacturing used across the design. As COO Dr Jack James Marlow put it: "You build confidence through thousands of decisions, measurements and tests along the way."
