Constellation Plan

Open Cosmos is turning its new funding into a constellation. Fresh from its EUR300m Series C, the UK-founded company has set out ConnectedCosmos: a sovereign European LEO network for government, institutional and commercial customers, using optical inter-satellite links. Open Cosmos says it can build a satellite a day, and it is already talking to launch providers for missions from late 2026 into mid-2027. Europe's next big space decisions have a date: 15 December, Rome. ESA Member State ministers meet for the Intermediate Ministerial Meeting to decide Europe's direction on low Earth orbit access after the ISS, and on a European presence on and around the Moon.
